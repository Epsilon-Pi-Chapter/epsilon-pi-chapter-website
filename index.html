Programs & Initiatives

The Epsilon Pi Chapter advances the Fraternity's national programs through year-round service, scholarship, and uplift across the Hampton Roads community.

Go-To-High School, Go-To-College

Brothers visit area high schools to mentor young men, share the college application process, and demonstrate that higher education is attainable for every student who pursues it.

A Voteless People is a Hopeless People

Voter registration drives, civic education events, and "Souls to the Polls" rideshares keep our campus and neighbors politically engaged at every level of government.

Project Alpha

Workshops with young men on responsibility, healthy relationships, and reproductive education — partnered with local schools and the March of Dimes.

Brother's Keeper & Service Hours

Soup kitchens, campus clean-ups, mentorship circles, and chapter-wide service projects that consistently exceed national hour requirements each semester.
